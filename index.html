The research

Technology with a human purpose.

The project combines biomedical signal acquisition, machine learning, embedded systems and digital fabrication to pursue a more accessible prosthetic solution.

EMG

Muscle-signal control

Acquire and process electromyography signals to translate residual muscle activity into useful hand commands.

AI

On-device intelligence

Develop machine-learning based gesture recognition that can operate on a compact embedded platform.

3D

Affordable fabrication

Use locally accessible components, sensors, motors and 3D printing to pursue a practical low-cost design.

↗

User-centred validation

Work with amputees and rehabilitation partners to understand usability, comfort, functionality and real-world needs.

⚙

Embedded systems

Integrate signal processing, control electronics, actuators and firmware into a working prosthetic platform.

♥

Independence matters

The long-term goal is to support everyday activities and improve independence for low-income amputees in Bangladesh.

Core objectives

From signals to a usable hand.

01
EMG acquisition

Develop an EMG signal acquisition system for robust, low-noise recording using locally available components.

02
Gesture recognition

Build a machine learning model for hand-gesture recognition and deploy it on a microcontroller.

03
Prosthetic development

Design a lightweight, durable and affordable prosthetic hand using 3D printing, sensors and servo motors.

04
Testing with amputees

Test and validate the prosthetic hand with amputees for usability, comfort and functionality.
