About the project
Affordable Myoelectric Prosthetic Hand Enabling Essential Daily Activities for Low-Income Amputees in Bangladesh — an ICSETEP Research & Development Grant Sub-Project

In Bangladesh, thousands of people lose a limb every year due to accidents, burns, and undiagnosed conditions, yet fewer than 1 in 10 amputees can access a functional prosthetic — imported myoelectric hands can cost more than 10 lakh BDT. This project designs and builds a low-cost, 3D-printed myoelectric prosthetic hand using local components: an EMG signal acquisition system, a machine-learning model for gesture recognition, and a lightweight, durable hand assembly — tested and validated with amputees in Bangladesh to restore independence in daily activities such as grasping, holding, and pointing.
